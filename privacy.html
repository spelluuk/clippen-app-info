Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-15

Clippen is a personal, non-commercial automation tool built and operated by a single individual for their own use. It is not a public service and does not have other end users.

What Clippen does

Clippen runs on the owner's own computer. Given a video the owner selects, it downloads the video, transcribes the audio locally, and uses this to pick and edit short vertical clips with burned-in captions. The owner can then post the resulting clips to their own TikTok account.

What data is processed

Clippen only processes video and audio content that the owner explicitly provides. It does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any other person, and it does not sell or share any data with advertisers.

Third-party services used

To do its job, Clippen sends transcript text to the Anthropic API (Claude) to help select which parts of a video to use, and uses TikTok's official Content Posting API solely to upload the resulting videos to the owner's own TikTok account. No other third parties are involved.
